Ball Adventure 3D

Published by Samir Dubey (SDCode) for Android

Effective 12 November 2026

Ball Adventure 3D is a free game from Samir Dubey (SDCode). Career and Endless are playable offline with a local save.

Coins and game progress

Earn Coins in Career, Endless and achievements. Spend them on eligible ball designs and optional powers within the game. Coins have no cash value and cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash. Your Coins and progress are stored in your device's game save.

Ads and the optional revive

Automatic ads may appear between runs for eligible profiles. When offered, you can choose to finish a rewarded video for one revive in the current Endless run. Ads and purchases are available to players aged 18 or older who confirm their age group.

Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a one-time Google Play purchase that removes automatic ads. Optional revive videos stay available when offered. Google Play shows the local price before checkout.

Use the same Google Play account and select Restore Purchases on the Remove Ads screen to restore the unlock. Use Google Play Help for payments and refunds.
