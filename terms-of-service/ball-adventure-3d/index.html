Ball Adventure 3D

Published by Samir Dubey (SDCode) for Android

Effective 11 November 2026

Ball Adventure 3D is a free game from Samir Dubey (SDCode). Career and Endless are available without buying coins or signing in.

Accounts and game progress

Where the online features are available, players aged 18 or older can create an account to use optional purchases and verified ad rewards. Keep your sign-in details private. Offline progress and earned coins remain on the device. The account syncs its own coin balance and eligible items.

Coins and optional ads

Coins are for use inside Ball Adventure 3D. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash. Where available, you can buy packs through Google Play or watch an optional ad for the reward shown in the game. The account service verifies purchases and ad rewards before granting them.

Ad-Free Year

Where offered, Ad-Free Year is an annual Google Play subscription that removes automatic interstitial ads while the subscription is active. Optional reward videos remain available when offered. Google Play shows the current local price and renewal terms before purchase. The subscription renews annually unless canceled through Google Play. Manage or cancel it in Google Play subscriptions.

To refresh access, sign in with the original game account and Google Play account, then select Refresh / Restore in the store. For payment or refund help, use Google Play Help.

Privacy and support
