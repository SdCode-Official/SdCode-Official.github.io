1 Information We Collect

The Tic Tac Toe — Themes app itself does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) from users.

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Player names entered in-game (stored in device SharedPreferences)
  • Game statistics — wins, losses, draws per difficulty level (stored in SQLite database on-device)
  • Selected theme preference (stored in device SharedPreferences)

We do not operate servers or databases that store your personal data. However, our third-party advertising partner (Google AdMob) may collect certain data as described below.

2 How We Use Information

The locally stored data (player names, game stats, theme) is used solely to:

  • Display your player name during gameplay
  • Track your game statistics to unlock new themes
  • Remember your theme preference between sessions

None of this data is shared with SDCode, third parties, or any external service by the app itself.

3 Third-Party Services — Google AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob is provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and process data including but not limited to:

  • Advertising ID (GAID / IDFA) — a resettable, anonymous device identifier
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • IP address (used to determine approximate location)
  • App usage data and interaction data with advertisements
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

This data is used by Google to serve relevant, targeted advertisements and to measure ad performance. Google's data processing is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Partner Guidelines
  • Google Advertising Policies

4 Advertising & Consent

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we are required under GDPR to obtain your consent before showing personalized advertisements.

When you first launch the app, you will be presented with a consent dialog powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You may:

  • Accept personalized ads — ads are tailored to your interests based on your profile
  • Reject personalized ads — you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized (contextual only)

If you are located in California, USA, you have the right to opt out of the "sale" of personal information under CCPA. You may manage your ad preferences through the Google settings on your device.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time via:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID
  • Google Ad Settings

5 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Tic Tac Toe — Themes is designed as a general audience game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designated as a children's app under Google Play's Families Policy. AdMob is configured to serve ads appropriate for general audiences.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us at the address below so we can take appropriate action.

6 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access — Request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure — Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing — Request that we limit how your data is used
  • Right to Data Portability — Request your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object — Object to processing of your data for direct marketing
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — Withdraw your consent to ad personalization at any time

Since all app data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise your right to erasure by simply uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored data.

For data processed by Google AdMob, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and use Google's privacy controls.

7 Data Security

We take data security seriously. All data generated by the app is stored locally on your device using Android's built-in security (app sandboxing, SharedPreferences, and SQLite). This data cannot be accessed by other apps on your device.

We do not transmit your app data over the internet. Network activity in the app is limited to AdMob's ad-loading network requests, which are handled securely by Google's infrastructure using HTTPS encryption.

8 Data Retention

Locally stored data (player names, game statistics, theme preference) is retained on your device for as long as the app is installed. This data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

For data collected by Google AdMob, retention periods are governed by Google's data retention policies. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Previous versions of this policy are available upon request by contacting us.
