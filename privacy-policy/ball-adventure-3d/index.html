Privacy Policy
Ball Adventure 3D

Published by Samir Dubey (SDCode) for Android

Effective 11 November 2026

Ball Adventure 3D can be played offline without an account. This policy also covers optional account, advertising and purchase features in releases where they are available. Request account deletion.

What stays on your device

The game saves Career progress, Endless records, earned coins, unlocked items and settings on your device. If you enter your age, the game saves the eligibility group rather than your exact age. Profiles under 18 do not initialize the game's account, advertising or purchase features. You can remove device-only saves by clearing the game's app data.

Optional accounts and purchases

Firebase Authentication processes your email address and password when you create or sign in to an account. Your email supports sign-in, verification and password resets. The app does not save your password. On Android it stores the sign-in refresh token using device encryption.

The game's service uses an account identifier, account coin balance, owned items and subscription status to sync purchases and verified rewards. It processes Google Play product identifiers, purchase tokens and purchase, renewal and refund details to verify orders, manage entitlements and prevent duplicate grants. Google Play handles checkout and payment details. Your offline Career save and earned coin balance are stored separately on your device.

Ads and privacy choices

When advertising is available, Google AdMob and its privacy tools may process IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. The game uses ad-unit and transaction identifiers and a reward ticket to verify optional reward videos. Where applicable, manage consent in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. Your Android settings also provide controls for the advertising ID.

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Service providers and retention

The game uses Google services for authentication, storage, advertising, billing and purchase verification. Account and purchase requests use HTTPS. We keep account data to provide the account and its features. After deletion, we retain limited account closure, purchase and reward verification records as needed to prevent fraud and repeat grants, process refunds and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Google Play may retain billing records under its own policies.

Delete your Ball Adventure 3D account

Email a deletion request to contact2sdcode@gmail.com. Send it from your account email address if you can, and identify the account you want deleted. We may verify ownership before processing the request. Keep your password out of the email. You can use this option without the game installed.

In versions with accounts, you can also sign in, open Coins & Ad-Free → Account → Delete Account, and confirm. Deletion removes your sign-in and clears the account balance, owned items and subscription access in the game. Device-only progress stays on your device. The limited records described above may be retained.

If you have an active subscription, cancel it in Google Play before deleting your game account. Deletion does not cancel Google Play billing.
