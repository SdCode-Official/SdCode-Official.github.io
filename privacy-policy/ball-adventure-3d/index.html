Privacy Policy
Ball Adventure 3D

Published by Samir Dubey (SDCode) for Android

Effective 12 November 2026

Ball Adventure 3D can be played offline without a game account. Google services handle optional ads and the one-time Remove Ads purchase on Android.

Game data on your device

The game saves your Career progress, Endless records, earned Coins, unlocked items and settings locally. The age screen stores an eligibility group on the device. Ads and purchases are available to players aged 18 or older who confirm their age group.

To erase the game's local data, open Ball Adventure 3D in your Android app settings and clear its storage. This resets the game's saved progress and settings. Android may back up app data according to your device settings.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles checkout, payment and refunds for Remove Ads. The game verifies and stores the signed receipt on your device. It checks current ownership with Google Play when it starts, resumes or you choose Restore Purchases. Use the Google Play account that bought the unlock to restore it on another device.

Manage billing data and payment requests with Google Play Help.

Ads and privacy choices

Google AdMob and its consent tools may collect and share IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google encrypts this data in transit. The game uses the completed video event to allow one optional revive in the current Endless run.

Where applicable, manage ad consent in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. Android settings provide controls for the advertising ID.
